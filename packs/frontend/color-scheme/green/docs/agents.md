## Colour scheme: green

- The palettes in `src/index.css` are shadcn/ui's slate in its green scheme: the primary colour, the sidebar's active item, the focus rings and the charts are green, everything else slate.
- `fabric upgrade` keeps `fabric.yaml`'s choices (blue, green and orange are the schemes), so setting another scheme there does not repaint the palettes; another scheme is a change of the project's choices, decided with a decision record of its own, not a hand edit.
